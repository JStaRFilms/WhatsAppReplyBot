Familiar first.
Assistant second.

Keep WhatsApp's spatial rhythm. Give Otherme its own charcoal and blue identity. The message history fills the screen. Assistant work stays beside the conversation it belongs to.

Keep the useful parts

  • Full-width inbox, stable avatar and timestamp lanes.
  • Compact contact header, quotes inside bubbles.
  • Bottom composer, no tabs inside contact chats.
  • Explicit draft review and acknowledged takeover.

Leave the noise out

No dashboard, giant AI hero, unreadable glass bubbles, fake call controls, encryption claims, or delivered/read ticks without evidence.

Quiet glass is optional

Same screen geometry. Near-opaque glass on chrome only. Opaque messages. Flat fallback for reduced transparency and battery-saving mode.

Product boundary

One owner, one WhatsApp account, Windows service on the same LAN. Never reply, Draft only, and bounded Auto-reply remain explicit. Unknown outcomes never get a blind retry.
