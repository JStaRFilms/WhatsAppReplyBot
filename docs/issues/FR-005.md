# FR-005: Confirmed autonomy and reactions

## Requirement

Autonomous actions require a confirmed conversation grant. The owner-approved
extension supersedes the original twenty-unit/twenty-four-hour ceiling, allowing
custom/unlimited counts and arbitrary/no expiry with explicit risk acceptance.
It does not loosen contact permissions, quiet hours, manual holds or send safety.

## Acceptance

- Auto mode alone is not a grant. A reviewed owner proposal must specify a
  positive count or explicitly unlimited, plus a future end time or no expiry.
- More than twenty actions, more than twenty-four hours, unlimited count or no
  expiry requires warning acknowledgment included in the confirmed payload hash.
- Reserve one unit per text/reaction component. Reaction plus text costs two;
  split bubbles cannot evade a finite limit. Unlimited grants still count units.
- Accepted handoffs consume capacity once. Definitive failures release
  reservations; unknown outcomes retain them and block blind retries.
- Owner pause, revocation, handover, quiet hours and finite expiry still gate
  dispatch. A temporary cooldown never renews the original grant or its budget.
- AI/model output cannot authorize or expand a grant. Never-reply permits no AI
  access. Draft-only requires owner approval, including for reactions.
- Fresh one-shot approvals during a grant use the same accounting. Oversized
  bundles cannot silently truncate to fit a remaining finite allowance.

## Contracts and verification

Grant maxUnits/expiresAt are nullable only for explicitly unbounded choices.
Successful/reserved units remain durable. See
[media and delegation](../features/Media_and_Delegation.md). Focused tests cover
finite and nullable combinations, warning gates, accounting and original grants
through cooldown/resume. Native indefinite-run behavior still needs owner use.

Media sending and attempts to bypass finite limits remain out of scope.
