# FR-006: Owner takeover, cooldown and resumption

## Requirement

Give the owner durable conversation control. This approved update supersedes the
original no-timer-resumption rule for temporary owner/composer pauses only.

## Acceptance

- Composer occupancy is acknowledged durably; typed text remains device-local.
- Nonempty drafts hold AI. Emptying a composer releases only its own hold after
  ten seconds. Device identities persist as long as their local drafts.
- A confirmed owner send starts or resets a five-minute cooldown. Original
  WhatsApp sends count once observed as verified live owner events.
- Pending or unknown sends do not become successful-send cooldowns. Replaying an
  accepted request does not reset its deadline without a new send.
- Takeover invalidates stale generation and cancels queued AI work. Temporary
  holds keep only the original unexpired grant and its remaining units.
- At expiry, an unanswered live message receives a draft only. Later incoming
  messages follow prior contact mode and original remaining allowance.
- Handover and explicit indefinite pause do not expire automatically. Resume now
  bypasses timed holds but cannot discard nonempty composers or unknown delivery.
- Clearing one device composer cannot release another device's unsent draft.
- No timer resets budgets, revives expired grants or bypasses never-reply.
- WhatsApp typing remains optional/unverified; already handed-off work may still
  deliver after pause acknowledgment.

## Contracts and verification

See [owner controls](../features/Owner_Control_and_Local_Drafts.md). Layered pause
state, device-scoped occupancy and catch-up identity survive restart. Fake-clock
checks cover deadlines, handover, indefinite pause, duplicate dispatch and grants.
Browser checks cover local draft restoration, occupancy privacy, history cache
and unread view watermarks. Live/native behavior still requires owner checks.
