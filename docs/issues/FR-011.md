# FR-011: Privacy and local data lifecycle

## Requirement

Protect local session/data storage, require consent for model export and let
the owner exclude, correct or delete AI-accessible data.

This is a MUS v1 requirement. Its product behavior is planned, not verified
by this documentation task.

## Acceptance

- Require explicit privacy consent before OpenRouter use. Explain selected data
  leaves the machine through OpenRouter and its provider; do not assume zero
  provider retention.
- Excluded contacts have no AI drafting, memory, style, interpretation or
  content-based model review. Exclusion cancels pending work and removes
  derived AI records.
- Apply initial configurable retention of 30 days for ordinary content/derived
  records and seven days for ordinary media/cache events. Honor earlier
  protocol expiry and view-once defaults.
- Local deletion removes content and derivatives, cancels context jobs and
  reports its limits. Keep only content-free evidence of unresolved delivery
  until reconciliation, then apply operational retention.
- Protect SQLite/session files with owner-only Windows permissions and
  production disk protection. SQLite is not encrypted by default. Backups are
  opt-in, encrypted and subject to deletion/expiry.
- Logs omit private text, phone identifiers, prompts, media, credentials,
  headers and pairing QR data. Test fixtures contain synthetic data only.

## Contracts

accounts model_consent_at/retention_days, contacts ai_excluded, media
delete_at, memory deletion and local runtime session references. Confirmed
exclusion/history deletion and memory correction endpoints.

Use [Assistant_Architecture.md](../features/Assistant_Architecture.md) as the
shared schema and API/event contract. See the
[requirements index](../Project_Requirements.md) for scope and precedence.

## Verification

Offline tests inspect fake model calls, redacted logs, exclusion/deletion
effects and expiry. Windows permissions and provider policy require separate
operational review; local deletion cannot prove provider deletion.

The evidence gates and test matrix are in
[Verification_Plan.md](../Verification_Plan.md). No live test is authorized
by this issue.

## Out of scope

Private-data collection in Genesis, customer compliance infrastructure,
plaintext committed sessions and claims of encrypted SQLite.
