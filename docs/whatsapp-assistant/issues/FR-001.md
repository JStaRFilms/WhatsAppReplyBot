# FR-001: Owner-authenticated messaging and connection state

## Requirement

Provide future Expo messaging backed by the Windows service. Support manual
text, quoted replies, image/sticker/GIF display, reactions, inline drafts and
honest connection/delivery states while ordinary WhatsApp remains usable.

This is a MUS v1 requirement. Its product behavior is planned, not verified
by this documentation task.

## Acceptance

- An authenticated owner can manually send in never_reply, draft_only and
  auto_reply modes, including excluded contacts. Manual sending applies
  takeover before queuing.
- Quotes and reaction targets resolve to the same account/conversation. Reject
  unknown or cross-contact targets without handoff.
- History preserves direction, authorship and missing-media state. Do not
  promise complete historical sync or full WhatsApp parity.
- Queued, accepted, delivered, read, failed, partial and unknown are distinct
  observations. Disconnection cannot be shown as an acknowledged pause or
  successful send.
- Reject unauthenticated/revoked devices and cross-account IDs. No WhatsApp
  content can authenticate an owner.

## Contracts

accounts, owner_devices, conversations, messages, media_items, reactions and
outbox. GET conversations/messages and POST manual-send; connection.changed,
message.upserted and delivery.changed.

Use [Assistant_Architecture.md](../features/Assistant_Architecture.md) as the
shared schema and API/event contract. See the
[requirements index](../Project_Requirements.md) for scope and precedence.

## Verification

Offline API/state tests prove local authorization and normalization. Later
approved Expo implementation checks display/playback and interaction behavior.
Device tests must establish history, media and delivery evidence.

The evidence gates and test matrix are in
[Verification_Plan.md](../Verification_Plan.md). No live test is authorized
by this issue.

## Out of scope

Expo UI during Genesis, full WhatsApp parity, voice/calls, status publishing
and autonomous media sends.
